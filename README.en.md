## What the guide covers

The first 14 chapters introduce Muse and explain materials, instructions, revisions, results, connected accounts, permissions, memory and recurring work. Eight case chapters follow. An optional final section explains models, tools and background tasks after readers have seen their practical uses.

Cases distinguish media tests, community experiences, creator reports and official examples. Each includes attribution and links, with selected quotations and Chinese translations where appropriate. The collection includes limits and unsuccessful attempts. Its 108 entries are not a claim of universal endorsement or independently reproduced success.

## Evidence and current limits

Sources were checked through **October 4, 2026**. The manuscript has received editorial and source-statement review; the illustrations have been visually checked. **The book’s authors have not performed the Muse workflows, tested Chinese-language performance or run reader trials.** Operational instructions follow official documentation; reported outcomes belong to their original sources. See [verification scope (Chinese)](docs/核验范围.md) and [source list (Chinese)](参考来源.md).

If useful, star the repository to find a scenario again. For release notifications, use **Watch → Custom → Releases**. Report corrections through [Issues](https://github.com/mali9527/muse-full-guide-for-beginners/issues), including the chapter, case and date, with personal details removed.
